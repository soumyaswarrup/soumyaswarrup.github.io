HOW THEY WORK AT A BOOTH

1. SCAN

A QR on the stand

Visitors play on their own phone in 30 to 60 seconds. No app, no install.

2. LEARN

Every obstacle is a real feature

Each run ends by explaining which product capability caught you, so the pitch lands while they're having fun.

3. UNLOCK

A comic as the reward

Score 10 to unlock the comic. Players sign up with a work email, which gives the team qualified leads and a live leaderboard for the big screen.

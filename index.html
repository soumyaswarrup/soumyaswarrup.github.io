HOW THEY WORK AT A BOOTH

1. SCAN

A QR on the stand

Visitors play on their own phone in 30 to 60 seconds. No app, no install.

2. LEARN

Every obstacle is a real feature

Each run ends by explaining which product capability caught you, so the pitch lands while they're having fun.

3. UNLOCK

A comic as the reward

Score 10 to unlock the comic. Players sign up with a work email, which gives the team qualified leads and a live leaderboard for the big screen.

ABOUT

I've spent the last few years since graduating in 2023 at the meeting point of security products and the people who buy them. At WhizHack my work spans GTM messaging, product documentation, compliance matrices for tenders, proposal writing and founder-adjacent work.

The games and comics come from one belief: people remember stories, not spec sheets. If someone gets caught by a decoy in a game, they understand deception technology better than any datasheet could teach them.
